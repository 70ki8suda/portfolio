# Brand Core

## Core Traits

- precise
- cinematic
- premium
- restrained
- post-digital
- luminous
- slightly strange

## Shared Brand Principle

All Nodex products should feel like members of one family, even when their
functions differ.

Theme changes should alter atmosphere, not identity.

## What Nodex Is Not

- not generic dashboard SaaS
- not gamer RGB UI
- not messy experimental collage
- not maximal ornamental futurism
- not sterile enterprise minimalism

## Non-Negotiables

- Information hierarchy must stay clear.
- Interaction surfaces must stay crisp and intentional.
- Typography must carry authority.
- Color must be controlled and atmospheric.
- Motion must feel designed, not decorative.
- Decorative accents must stay peripheral.

## Rose In The Machine

Nodex may occasionally place a small poetic artifact at the edge of the screen.

This artifact is not functional.

It exists to create emotional depth and memorability.

Rules:

- use sparingly
- keep it outside primary task flows
- never let it reduce clarity
- one quiet accent is stronger than repeated motifs
