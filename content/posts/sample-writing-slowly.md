> **This is a sample article.** Replace or delete it when you publish your own writing.

This blog has no database, no admin screen, and no external CMS. Each post is a Markdown file in the repository, reviewed and committed like any other change.

That constraint is deliberate. Writing in the same place as the code keeps the history in one timeline, and a plain text file will still open in twenty years.

## What the site does for you

- Lists published posts, newest first.
- Lets readers filter by tag.
- Hides anything marked `draft: true`, both from the list and at its direct URL.

## What it leaves to you

Everything else: what to write, and when to slow down.
